Travel services

Flight Tickets

Search, book and ticket flights for walk-in customers, online travellers and B2B agents.

  • Fare search with filters for airline, stops and baggage
  • PNR creation, ticket issue, reissue, void and refund
  • Fare rules, baggage and seat information
  • Agent-wise markup, commission and service fee
  • Group bookings and name changes
  • E-ticket and invoice sent automatically
Learn more

Hotel Booking

Sell your contracted hotels next to supplier inventory with clear room-level pricing.

  • Hotel, room type and rate plan management
  • Allotments, stop-sell and blackout dates
  • Child policy, meal plans and cancellation rules
  • Supplier API and own contracts side by side
  • Room-level markup by market or agent
  • Hotel vouchers with your branding
Learn more

Hajj & Umrah

Run pilgrim operations from registration to return, with packages and payments in one place.

  • Pilgrim registration with passport and photo
  • Package builder: flights, Makkah & Madinah hotels, transport
  • Group and room allocation
  • Visa and document status per pilgrim
  • Instalment payments and due reminders
  • Mua'llim, guide and service provider records
Learn more

Visa Processing

Organise every application with requirements, documents and stages your team can track.

  • Country and visa-type requirement lists
  • Document upload and checklist per applicant
  • Application stages with responsible staff
  • Embassy appointment and submission dates
  • Automatic SMS, email and WhatsApp status updates
  • Visa fee, service charge and profit report
Learn more

Tour Packages

Design packages once and sell them on your website, to agents and over WhatsApp.

  • Day-by-day itinerary builder with photos
  • Fixed departures and seat inventory
  • Adult, child and group pricing
  • Inclusions, exclusions and terms
  • Shareable PDF brochures
  • Supplier costing and package profit
Learn more

Transport Booking

Handle airport transfers, intercity transport and rentals with vehicles and drivers.

  • Vehicle and driver management
  • Zone and route-based pricing
  • Airport pick-up and drop scheduling
  • Driver assignment and trip sheets
  • Add transport to any package or booking
  • Vendor payables for hired vehicles
Learn more

CRM

Every customer, lead and conversation in one profile.

  • Customer profiles with travel history and documents
  • Lead pipeline from inquiry to booking
  • Follow-up reminders and activity log
  • Lead source tracking: website, WhatsApp, Facebook, walk-in
  • Customer segments for offers
  • Birthday and passport-expiry reminders
Learn more

Sales Management

Turn inquiries into confirmed bookings and see how your team performs.

  • Quotations with multiple options
  • Convert a quote to a booking in one click
  • Sales targets by staff and branch
  • Conversion and win-rate reports
  • Discount approval rules
  • Commission for sales staff
Learn more

Finance & Accounting

Double-entry accounting that updates itself with every booking and payment.

  • Chart of accounts, journals and ledgers
  • Customer, agent and supplier balances
  • Bank, cash, bKash and Nagad accounts
  • Profit and loss, balance sheet and trial balance
  • Multi-currency with exchange gain or loss
  • Branch and cost-centre accounting
Learn more

HR & Employees

Manage your team, attendance and payroll in the same system.

  • Employee profiles and documents
  • Attendance and leave management
  • Payroll with allowances and deductions
  • Roles and permissions per staff member
  • Staff targets and incentives
  • Branch and department structure
Learn more

Help Desk & Support

Resolve customer issues quickly and keep a full history.

  • Support tickets from any channel
  • Assignment to staff or teams
  • Priority and SLA tracking
  • Linked to the customer's bookings
  • Internal notes and attachments
  • Satisfaction feedback

Expense Management

Track every taka spent, by branch, trip or category.

  • Expense categories and budgets
  • Receipt upload
  • Approval workflow
  • Trip and group expenses
  • Branch-wise cost reports
  • Posted to accounts automatically

Invoice & Payment

Bill customers and agents correctly and follow up on dues.

  • Branded invoices and money receipts
  • Partial and instalment payments
  • Online payment links
  • Due and overdue reminders
  • Refunds and credit notes
  • Agent wallet and deposit top-ups
Learn more

Reports & Analytics

See what's selling, what's profitable and what's owed.

  • Sales by service, staff, branch and agent
  • Profit per booking and per package
  • Receivable and payable aging
  • Cash flow and daily cash book
  • Airline and supplier performance
  • Export to Excel and PDF

Tasks & Workflows

Assign work, set deadlines and automate routine handovers.

  • Tasks linked to bookings and customers
  • Due dates and reminders
  • Team task boards
  • Automatic tasks on booking events
  • Checklists for visa and Hajj files
  • Activity timeline per file
