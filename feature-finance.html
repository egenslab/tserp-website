Overview

Finance & Accounting

Travel agency accounting software with full double-entry bookkeeping. Every booking, payment, refund and supplier bill posts automatically to the right ledgers, so your books are always current.

Customer, agent and supplier balances, bank and wallet accounts, multi-currency gains and losses and branch P&L are available at any time, ready for your auditor.

Key capabilities

Double-entry accounting that updates itself with every booking and payment.

Chart of accounts, journals and ledgers
Customer, agent and supplier balances
Bank, cash, bKash and Nagad accounts
Profit and loss, balance sheet and trial balance
Multi-currency with exchange gain or loss
Branch and cost-centre accounting

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I still need separate accounting software?

No. TravelSuite ERP includes the ledger, balance sheet and P&L. You can still export to QuickBooks or Xero if your accountant prefers.

Does it support multiple currencies?

Yes. Sell and buy in USD, SAR, AED, MYR, BDT and more, with exchange gains and losses recorded.

Can I see profit per booking?

Yes. Cost, selling price, fees and margin are stored on every booking.

Works with every TravelSuite module

Bookings, customers, payments and accounts stay in one database, so this module shares data with everything else automatically.
