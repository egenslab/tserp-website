Flight Tickets

Sell air tickets to walk-in customers, online travellers and your agent network from one screen. Every ticket you issue updates the customer's file, the agent's balance and your accounts at the same time.

Request a demo Chat with TravelSuiteAvailable now

Why agencies use it

  • No manual entry between GDS and accounts
  • Correct markup for every agent and channel
  • Refunds and reissues tracked to the last taka
Key capabilities

Search, book and ticket flights for walk-in customers, online travellers and B2B agents.

Fare search with filters for airline, stops and baggage
PNR creation, ticket issue, reissue, void and refund
Fare rules, baggage and seat information
Agent-wise markup, commission and service fee
Group bookings and name changes
E-ticket and invoice sent automatically
