The ERP part

Every booking posts to your books automatically

Most booking scripts stop at checkout. In Travel Suite ERP, the moment a ticket is issued the invoice, supplier payable, agent ledger and commission are already recorded.

  • Accounts Double-entry ledger, invoices, receipts, VAT, multi-currency and bank reconciliation.
  • Agents Sub-agent tree, credit limits, deposits, commission and statement of account.
  • Suppliers Contracts, payables, BSP/ADM reconciliation and settlement reports.
  • CRM Leads, quotations, follow-ups and WhatsApp/email templates.
  • Staff & branches Role permissions, branch-wise P&L, targets and staff incentives.

FAQ

Questions agencies ask us

Is this a booking script or a full ERP?

Both. You get a customer booking website and a complete back office with accounts, agent and supplier management, CRM and reports, sharing one database.

Can I install it on my own server?

Yes. Choose our managed cloud, or buy a self-hosted license and we install it on your VPS free of charge.

Do I need my own supplier API accounts?

For GDS and bedbank connections you sign directly with the supplier and we connect your credentials. You can also sell your own inventory without any API.

Which payment gateways can I use?

SSLCommerz, bKash, Nagad, Stripe, PayPal, Razorpay, bank transfer and agent wallet. Other gateways can be added on request.

Can you customise features for us?

Yes. Themes are fully editable, and Enterprise plans include source code access and custom development.

How long does it take to go live?

Most agencies go live in 7 to 14 days, including branding, domain, payment gateway and staff training.
