FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is TSERP a booking script or a full ERP?

Both. You get a customer-facing booking website and a complete back office with accounting, agent and supplier management, CRM and reports — all on the same database.

Can I host it on my own server?

Yes. Choose our managed Cloud plan, or buy a self-hosted license and install it on your own VPS. We offer free installation with every license.

Do I need my own supplier API credentials?

For GDS and bedbank integrations you sign a contract directly with the supplier; we connect your credentials to TSERP. You can also sell your own manual inventory with no API at all.

Which payment gateways are supported?

Over 30 gateways including Stripe, PayPal, Razorpay, SSLCommerz, bKash, Nagad and bank transfer. New gateways can be added on request.

Can I customise the design and features?

Yes. Themes are fully customisable, and Enterprise plans include source code access and custom development.

How long does it take to go live?

Most agencies go live within 7–14 days, including branding, domain setup, payment gateway and training.
